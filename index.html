Strategy Board Game Designer

Dean Morris

I design gateway-plus and mid-weight euro games built around spatial puzzles. Most build upward as well as outward, using tactile 3D components to give them real presence on the table: games that pull people over to watch, and reward them when they sit down.

Pacific Rails Inc. and Florence are published, and Highlands goes to crowdfunding in 2027. Everything else is a prototype, and the artwork shown for them is placeholder.

Australian, living in London. I'm at UK Games Expo and Essen Spiel every year to pitch in person. Full details of every game are below: sell sheets, rules, links to TTS and videos. Some of them are playable online in your web browser.
