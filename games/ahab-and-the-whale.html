Ahab and the Whale

In Development

Lead and win, go forward. Lead and lose, go backwards.

Players 2
Play Time 10 Min
Age 10+
Weight 1.8 / 5
Status In Development

Ahab and the Whale is a tug-of-war at sea, played with cards. One player is Captain Ahab, the other is the white whale. Every trick drags the whale a step across the sea chart: Ahab hauls it in towards his harpoon, while the Whale pulls it out to open sea.

The suit you lead is a compass wind: North, East, South or West. It picks the line the whale moves along. Lead and win, and the whale goes forward with the wind. Lead and lose, and it goes backwards. So the best lead is sometimes one you mean to lose.

Can't follow suit? You lose the trick, but the whale moves the way of the card you throw. After twelve tricks the chase is over: if the whale is inside harpoon range, Ahab strikes; if it is on the open sea around the rim, the whale swims free.

There are no trumps, no bidding and no scoring to track: the whale's position is the score. It is a short, tense two-player duel on a public-domain classic, with two asymmetric roles balanced in bot simulation and now heading to the table.
