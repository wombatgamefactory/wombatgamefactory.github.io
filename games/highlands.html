Highlands

Signed

Co Designed with Juan Hernández Sánchez. A card-driven engine building euro game set in the Scottish highlands.

Players 2–4
Play Time 90–120 Min
Age 14+
Status Signed - In Production

Help bring the wilderness back to life in Highlands, a conservation-themed card-driven engine builder set in Scotland. The game features over 150 cards, each beautifully illustrated by a range of talented artists.

Carefully manage your hand of cards as they determine both the actions you can take on your turn, and the species you'll introduce to Scotland, forming a tableau of cards reflecting the food chains present in Scotland. Access upgrades to unlock more powerful actions, allowing you to restore terrain and activate the abilities of the species in your tableau.

Search for the scoring mechanisms that best suit your engine to maximise the points you score for conserving and rewilding Scotland's natural environment.

Highlands is part of Alley Cat Games' advanced games line, which includes titles such as Ada's Dream, Baghdad and Calimala.
