Daruma Drama

In Development

Fall seven times, stand up eight.

Players 3–5
Play Time 30–40 Min
Age 10+
Weight 1.8 / 5
Status In Development

Daruma Drama is a trick-taking game built on the Japanese Daruma doll, which is weighted so that however often it is knocked over, it rolls back upright. The proverb that goes with it says: fall seven times, stand up eight. In this game, falling is how you get ahead.

Every card you lose a trick with lies sideways in front of you, knocked over. Your oldest fallen card is your own personal trump, so each player has a different one. Win a trick and you stand back up: your fallen cards are banked as points, but you give up your trump until you fall again.

Your line of fallen cards holds three, and everyone can see who is about to rise. That makes leading to lose a real choice, and the timing of your rise the heart of the game. Rise early and you bank little; fall a fourth time and your oldest card is knocked off, taking your trump with it.

The deck has 55 cards in five colours, each colour with its own bird so the game is friendly to colour-blind players. It comes with player aids and a score pad. It plays with three to five players in about 30 to 40 minutes, and is now heading to the table.
