AI Wants You Dead

In Development

Everyone can see whether you're human. Except you.

Players 2–5
Play Time 10–15 Min
Age 8+
Cards 52
Status In Development

AI Wants You Dead is a comedy card game about a very helpful AI. It has checked the room, and it is confident that everyone in it is human. It is not. Some of you are carrying a Rogue AI, and your friends can see who. You can't.

You hold your hand backwards. Your code faces the table, so everyone else sees your symbols and you see only the coloured backs. On your turn you Upgrade (draw a card and keep it face up in your cache, or slip it into your left neighbour's code), Debug (flip one of your own code cards and delete it), or Compile.

To compile, say "I'm not a robot!" The table checks your cache and your code together. If no symbol appears twice and no Rogue AI is hiding in your code, you bank your cache for a point a card, and big banks claim bonus cards. If not, it's a compile error: your cache is wiped, and nobody tells you why. Talk is free and lies are fine. Only the check must be truthful.

The game has 52 cards: 36 Parts in four colours (Killer Robots, Chatbots, Smart Home and Self-Driving), each with nine symbols from i to 404, plus 4 Rogue AIs, 6 bonus cards and 6 rules cards. Every Part has its own joke, the AI cheerfully getting the maths wrong: "π = 3.2. I have checked this." It plays with two to five players in 10 to 15 minutes, and teaches in two. It is in development.
